## Revision rule
Use this structure in the conversation during planning. Discuss feedback without rewriting the entire proposal on every reply or saving draft versions. Once discussion is settled, consolidate the proposal and obtain explicit approval. Then save and verify one versioned plan section in the phase/work-item page before implementation; no separate go-ahead is required. Inspect prior versions and increment for an approved replacement. Preserve the previous approved baseline in a versioned section or toggle on the same page and update current plan references. Material changes to scope, acceptance, contracts, or approach repeat this process before dependent work. Routine implementation choices within this plan may proceed.
